Core Philosophy

The Shri Shambhu Dashnam Avhan Akhada stands on the ancient principles of Shaivism and Sanatan Dharma, traceably rooted in devotion to Lord Shiva and Lord Dattatreya. It upholds spiritual discipline, protection of righteousness (Dharma), and self-realization through ascetic practices and selfless devotion.

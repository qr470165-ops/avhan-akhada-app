🔱 SHREE SHAMBHU PANCH DASHNAM AVAHAN AKHADA
Sanatan Dharma
"HAR HAR MAHADEV — ETERNAL STRENGTH IN DEVOTION"
🔱 Core Philosophy

The Shree Shambhu Panch Dashnam Avahan Akhada represents ancient traditions of Shaivism and Sanatan Dharma. Rooted in ultimate devotion to Lord Shiva and Lord Dattatreya, it emphasizes intense spiritual discipline (Sadhana), righteousness (Dharma), and self-realization through inner strength and selfless service.

Sacred Quote
"Perform duty without expectation of reward."
Category: Karma Yoga
🛡️ Ancient Lineage & Duty
  • • Spiritual Guardian: Preserving traditional Shaivite rituals and monastic order.
  • • Sadhana & Meditation: Encouraging self-mastery, detachment, and unwavering devotion.
  • • Dharma Protection: Standing firm as protector of Vedic knowledge and spiritual truths.
